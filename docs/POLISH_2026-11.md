# RAPIPPO polish after the jam (from 2026-11-10)

Started from `feature/story-jam-02` (three stages and FREE RUN) on the owner's word: make it richer to look at, gentler to learn, and clear to a first-time player. Player feedback behind it: "the visuals are too plain" and "the difficulty should rise more gently". The owner left the look to the author; neither `concept/ART_UX_DIRECTION.md` (2026-11-01) nor the later "美術方針 v1" note binds this work, though both were read.

## Round 2: the edge of a hole (2026-11-10)

Owner: the judgement at a hole's edge is severe and first-time players drop out there. A landing was judged at the hero's centre, which had to be 8px inside the drawn rock, while its foot is 28px wide: one pixel out and it fell with half its foot still on the ground.

- **Rule.** In STORY, the workshop and the clock tower a landing up to `EDGE_GRACE` = 10px beyond the lit top holds (2px past the drawn rock). In the two indoor stages only at edges with a rim, the ones that drop into a hole. Mechanisms are unchanged: a tooth that is down, a hand off its beat, used sand, an unwoken plate and the growing end of green's bridge still drop the hero.
- **Seen.** Held by the edge alone, the hero leans out over the hole, rocks back and stands, and a few crumbs fall (`land` events carry `edge: -1 | 1`). Not with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **FREE RUN is unchanged**: its lit top is still exactly the footing, as its records and DAILY courses assume.
- **Measured** (scripted): on STORY's clean route, inputs from the same states fall 23.3% of the time instead of 31.0%, a quarter of the falls gone, about the same in every chapter. Workshop rooms' safe share: tongue drum 0.89 to 0.96, strings 0.68 to 0.76, woodwind 0.60 to 0.72, bellows 0.48 to 0.59, cymbals 0.69 to 0.84. The tower barely moves (its hard hops are about the mechanisms' timing, not edges).
- **Lessons in STORY's first chapter still teach what they did**: SLOW alone clears lesson one and falls in two and three; FAST alone falls in one and two. FAST alone clears lesson three, with and without the grace (STORY_JAM02.md says it falls there; it did not before this change either).
- One Node test changed: the respawn test now falls with SLOW alone, since twelve FAST hops after the second part no longer fall.

## Round 3: FAST is the world, and gentler first halves (2026-11-11)

**The hero only hops.** The owner's original idea: Rapippo just hops, and the player changes how fast the world goes by. So the afterimage on the hero at FAST is gone. Instead sixteen near stars slide past and stretch into short streaks at FAST (`drawNearStars`, STORY and FREE RUN; eased, and not with `prefers-reduced-motion`).

**Rooms that jumped now start gently.** No rule changed, only what the first half of a room asks:

| Workshop, bellows | every push plate 214px | 150px for the first six plates, 182px for the next six, 214px for the last four |

Kept, and tested: holding SLOW or FAST alone still fails in every mechanism room (the hourglass's first slabs over holes were chosen so that FAST alone still falls there), every lantern reaches the next on each of the four beats, every gear is reachable.

Safe share of simple inputs, before the edge grace → now: mainspring 0.64 → 0.76, hourglass 0.57 → 0.69, hands 0.65 → 0.68, woodwind 0.60 → 0.72, bellows 0.48 → 0.59. The machine room reads 0.74 → 0.71 with twice as many hard hops on the solver's new route; it was not edited, and the measure depends on which beat the route arrives on, so this may be the route and not the room. ## Round 6: together again, the friends call and you answer (2026-11-21)

The jam build is submitted; from here the owner wants free brush-up (STORY_JAM02.md's freeze from 2026-11-03 no longer applies). The stretch after the reunion was "a lively stretch for the three-part ensemble" with nothing to do. Now it is one.

- **The call.** Once the four are together (rescue chapter, up to `COURSE.ensembleEnd`), on beats one and three of the song one friend in turn (blue, green, red) calls with two notes in its own voice and throws a light onto the ground two touchdowns ahead. The light waits there: a ring as wide as the reach that answers it, a short beam, one pip per touchdown left.
- **The answer.** Landing within `NOTE_REACH` = 22px of it on that beat answers: a rising three-note reply in the friend's voice, pitched to the chord on that beat, a ring in its colour and a thread of light back to the friend. Missed, the light just fades. Nothing is lost and nothing is counted on screen; `run.notes.answered` keeps the number.
- **Where the lights go.** Wanted reaches cycle through 380, 220, 310, 250, 400, 235, 290px, each moved to the nearest spot that two hops can reach with both touchdowns on rock (`noteSpot`). So neither SLOW nor FAST alone answers them all; a player who aims answers every one.
- **Measured** (scripted, from the rescue's last lantern): aiming answers 7 of 7 with no fall; a safe route leaning on SLOW answers 5 of 11 by chance, leaning on FAST 0 of 4.

Checked: 168 Node tests (one new: calls on the song's beats one and three, in turn, only once together; each answerable; neither SLOW nor FAST alone answers all), 34 STORY browser tests, the 11 real Web Audio tests. ## Round 11: blue wakes the plain (2026-11-21)

Past the dawn the plain (twelve stones, about twenty seconds) looked the same from start to end: nothing for blue's new light to do. Now six crystal spires stand on every other stone (`COURSE.spires`, 52 to 104px tall, three shards each). Dark glass at first, with the face towards Earth catching a little of its light, so they are seen ahead. On the touchdown that brings the hero within `SPIRE_WAKE` = 180px, with blue whole and following, a spire wakes: lit from inside, a ring climbing it, a thin ray up, then breathing softly on each beat. Each one rings a bell on a tone of that beat's chord, one step further up the chord than the last (`spireTones`), so crossing the plain plays a slow rising arpeggio over blue's tune. Nothing to do but go on: the plain stays the rest it was. Same ground as before.

Checked: 171 Node tests (one new: each spire once and in order, the pitches climb, a later start remembers them), 34 STORY browser tests, the 11 real Web Audio tests, captures across the plain (`node tests/browser/capture-coop.mjs plain`).
