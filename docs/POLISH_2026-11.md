# RAPIPPO polish after the jam (from 2026-11-10)

Started from `feature/story-jam-02` (three stages and FREE RUN) on the owner's word: make it richer to look at, gentler to learn, and clear to a first-time player. Player feedback behind it: "the visuals are too plain" and "the difficulty should rise more gently". The owner left the look to the author; neither `concept/ART_UX_DIRECTION.md` (2026-11-01) nor the later "美術方針 v1" note binds this work, though both were read.

## Round 1: the moon itself (2026-11-10)

`public/src/scenery.mjs` is new: one drawing module for the places that travel across the moon. STORY and FREE RUN use it; the workshop and the clock tower are indoors and keep their own art.

| Piece | What changed |
|---|---|
| Sky | Deep blue falling to a coloured horizon; 166 stars at several depths, a few that twinkle, a faint galaxy band, a meteor about every 19s. No twinkle or meteor with `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| Earth | The title's shaded Earth (land, cloud, a lit side, a rim of air) is now the one seen in play, with a halo. Repainted once a second to a small canvas. |
| Ranges | Three silhouettes instead of 28px blocks: palest far away, nearly black up close, each lit along its crest. Now and then something left on the plain (rocks, a mast with a lamp, a dish, a crate). |
| Ground | Blocks of rock with layers and buried stones that darken with depth. The top six rows stay one flat colour: where to land reads as before. |
| STORY only | Each chapter has its own horizon (night, violet for the rescue, warm at the base). Landing dust that slides along the ground, a shadow under the hero, a thin afterimage at FAST. In the dark first chapter, crystals in the ceiling glint on each touchdown and dust hangs in the hero's light. |
| FREE RUN | Same sky, ranges and rock. The far side still starts dark and the sky opens with Earth's glow. Footing colours, the pit colour and the landing sparks are unchanged (browser tests read them). |

Not changed: physics, timing, courses, collision, sound, BEST keys, DAILY seeds.

The stage select sits under the same sky (stars, Earth, ranges). New on it: one picture of the hop (let go lands near, hold lands far), each stage's length and subject, and a badge for a saved or finished stage, read from what the stages save themselves (STORY now writes `hoppod.story.v1.cleared` at its end card). The first stage is the one lit; the workshop's larger gold card is gone.

Checked: 163 Node tests; all 104 browser tests after the art change; the entry and itch.io ZIP tests (12) again after the select change.

## Round 2: the edge of a hole (2026-11-10)

Owner: the judgement at a hole's edge is severe and first-time players drop out there. A landing was judged at the hero's centre, which had to be 8px inside the drawn rock, while its foot is 28px wide: one pixel out and it fell with half its foot still on the ground.

- **Rule.** In STORY, the workshop and the clock tower a landing up to `EDGE_GRACE` = 10px beyond the lit top holds (2px past the drawn rock). In the two indoor stages only at edges with a rim, the ones that drop into a hole. Mechanisms are unchanged: a tooth that is down, a hand off its beat, used sand, an unwoken plate and the growing end of green's bridge still drop the hero.
- **Seen.** Held by the edge alone, the hero leans out over the hole, rocks back and stands, and a few crumbs fall (`land` events carry `edge: -1 | 1`). Not with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **FREE RUN is unchanged**: its lit top is still exactly the footing, as its records and DAILY courses assume.
- **Measured** (scripted): on STORY's clean route, inputs from the same states fall 23.3% of the time instead of 31.0%, a quarter of the falls gone, about the same in every chapter. Workshop rooms' safe share: tongue drum 0.89 to 0.96, strings 0.68 to 0.76, woodwind 0.60 to 0.72, bellows 0.48 to 0.59, cymbals 0.69 to 0.84. The tower barely moves (its hard hops are about the mechanisms' timing, not edges).
- **Lessons in STORY's first chapter still teach what they did**: SLOW alone clears lesson one and falls in two and three; FAST alone falls in one and two. FAST alone clears lesson three, with and without the grace (STORY_JAM02.md says it falls there; it did not before this change either).
- One Node test changed: the respawn test now falls with SLOW alone, since twelve FAST hops after the second part no longer fall.

## Round 3: FAST is the world, and gentler first halves (2026-11-11)

**The hero only hops.** The owner's original idea: Rapippo just hops, and the player changes how fast the world goes by. So the afterimage on the hero at FAST is gone. Instead sixteen near stars slide past and stretch into short streaks at FAST (`drawNearStars`, STORY and FREE RUN; eased, and not with `prefers-reduced-motion`).

**Rooms that jumped now start gently.** No rule changed, only what the first half of a room asks:

| Room | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Tower, mainspring | four coils 70 to 90px wide from the start | first two coils 170 and 150px wide, each with a 360 to 380px landing; then coils of 90 to 110px; the second half is unchanged |
| Tower, hourglass | slabs over holes from the first step | first three slabs set in solid floor (two narrower than a SLOW hop, so they can only be watched crumbling; one where SLOW can land twice); then slabs of 110 to 170px over holes; the second half is unchanged |
| Tower, hands | first hands 180, 190, 200px | 136, 156, 180px: FAST gets on and almost any hop gets off. Still longer than FAST's 128.7px hop off the cog, so FAST on four always reaches the hand |
| Workshop, woodwind | wide gaps of 104 and 112px among the first eight | long tones grow: 72, 80, 88, then 96 to 112px |
| Workshop, bellows | every push plate 214px | 150px for the first six plates, 182px for the next six, 214px for the last four |

Kept, and tested: holding SLOW or FAST alone still fails in every mechanism room (the hourglass's first slabs over holes were chosen so that FAST alone still falls there), every lantern reaches the next on each of the four beats, every gear is reachable.

Safe share of simple inputs, before the edge grace → now: mainspring 0.64 → 0.76, hourglass 0.57 → 0.69, hands 0.65 → 0.68, woodwind 0.60 → 0.72, bellows 0.48 → 0.59. The machine room reads 0.74 → 0.71 with twice as many hard hops on the solver's new route; it was not edited, and the measure depends on which beat the route arrives on, so this may be the route and not the room. The bellows' number understates real play: the measure only holds FAST from take-off, and a push plate wants FAST at touchdown.

## Round 4: the hands' signal (2026-11-11)

The four lamps before a clock hand did not help: the fourth lit on the very take-off that had to be FAST, the step before it ("three lit") looked like the two before, and they sat under the floor and on a post behind the hero. The rule is unchanged; what tells it is new.

- **A starting light over the pivot**, where the eyes are while waiting on the cog: three small amber lamps count the landings in, the third brighter and pulsing (the next landing), then one big lamp with an arrow, lit only for the hop that has to be FAST. The last step is a different shape, so it is seen and not counted.
- **Where the bridge will lie**: a dashed outline one landing ahead, a lit strip through the FAST hop.
- **The cog** shows ◀ ◀ while it carries the hero back and ▶ ▶ for the FAST hop. The world says go; nothing is drawn on the hero.
- **Heard**: within 260px before a hand each landing ticks with its lamp (C, C, E) and the big lamp rings (high C) on the take-off. Played ahead on the beat clock; measured in the browser on whole beats, 0.78s apart, the chime one beat before the hand lies flat.
- Station line and hint now say "big lamp" instead of "four lamps". Not shown or played with the assist on (the hands hold still there).

Checked: 163 Node tests, the 15 tower browser tests, four captured beats in a row. Not played by a person.
